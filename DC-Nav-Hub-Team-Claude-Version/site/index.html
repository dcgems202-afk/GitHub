About

About & Important Notice

The DC Home & Rental Navigation Hub provides general educational and navigation information and links to third-party resources.

It is an independent prototype and is not a DC government agency, housing provider, broker, lawyer, social worker, housing counselor, lender, insurer, or emergency service.

It does not provide legal, financial, real-estate, insurance, medical, or emergency advice.

It does not determine eligibility, availability, funding, placement, approval, or outcomes.

Programs and website information can change.

Confirm all current requirements directly on the linked organization’s official website.

External websites control their own content, accessibility, security, and privacy practices.

Privacy statement

This prototype does not require an account and should not collect names, contact information, financial details, disability documentation, housing documents, application data, or other sensitive personal information.

Your selections are used only to organize the starting points shown on this device.
