## Posting 5: Project purpose, features to keep, and content rules

PROJECT PURPOSE

This app is a plain-language navigation and education hub.

It helps DC renters, prospective renters, homebuyers, homeowners,
and people with urgent housing concerns understand what kind of
resource may fit their situation and then visit the responsible
organization's official website.

The app does not determine eligibility, promise assistance,
submit applications, assign counselors, provide live housing
inventory, or replace any agency or professional.


KEEP THESE FEATURES

- Keep the current name, DC visual identity, warm cream background,
  navy and terracotta palette, and mobile-first layout.

- Keep the three main paths:

  Find a place to rent

  Buy or own a home

  Get housing help

- Keep the short Education & Preparation cards.

- They should remain easy to scan, with a brief heading,
  3–5 bullets at most, and a highlighted
  "Your next action" box.

- Keep multi-select choices and the results-based
  navigation approach.

- Keep the existing rental-scam warning card, subject to
  the content and accessibility rules.


NON-NEGOTIABLE CONTENT RULES

1. Do not display phone numbers anywhere in the app.

   Do not add telephone numbers from linked pages,
   even if they are visible in source material.


2. Do not display staff emails, street addresses, or contact
   details that will need frequent maintenance.

   The primary action is a descriptive link to the
   official website.


3. Use only the exact URLs supplied for the resources.

   Do not invent, autocomplete, or substitute URLs.


4. Never state or imply that a user qualifies,
   will receive funding,
   will get an appointment,
   will be assigned a counselor,
   will receive housing,
   or will obtain a particular outcome.


5. Avoid fixed income limits, dollar amounts, wait times,
   program availability, deadlines, and benefit amounts
   unless the app is reading them from a maintained live source.

   Instead say:

   "Requirements, funding, and availability can change.
   Confirm current details on the official website."


6. Do not call every kind of one-on-one help
   "housing counseling."


7. Do not refer to DACL Safe at Home as a
   security-camera program.

   It is an accessibility and fall-prevention
   home-modification program.

   Security-camera resources are separate OVSJG programs.


8. The vacant/blighted-property dashboard is an
   information and research tool.

   Do not imply that a listed property is for sale,
   available to rent,
   safe to enter,
   or available for acquisition.


9. Do not turn user selections into eligibility screening.

   Selections only personalize suggested starting points.


10. Do not add a login, account, database, payment system,
    chat intake, document upload, or collection of
    personal information.


PREVENT DUPLICATE RESOURCES

- Maintain one record per organization.

- Maintain separate program or resource records when one
  organization operates several genuinely different programs.

  For example:

  DHCD is one organization.

  HPAP, EAHP, SFRRP, Housing Counseling Services,
  and the Homeownership CBO directory are separate
  resources connected to DHCD.

- A resource may appear in several result pathways through tags,
  but it must not be copied into separate duplicate records.

- If the same resource matches several selections,
  show it once and combine its relevant reason labels.

- Do not merge different programs merely because
  they share a parent agency.


IMPORTANT LINK/BUTTON RULE

Do not use buttons that only say:

"Learn more"

"Click here"

or

"Visit website"

Use descriptive buttons that tell the person
where the link is taking them.

External links must open in a new tab and use
safe external-link handling.

EDUCATION & PREPARATION CARDS

Keep these cards short.

Each card should contain no more than five bullets
and one “Your next action” sentence.


CARD: BEFORE APPLYING FOR A RENTAL

- Ask for the written screening criteria before paying
  an application fee.

- Confirm rent, deposit, utilities, move-in costs,
  and any refundable or nonrefundable fees.

- Prepare identification, income documentation,
  rental history, and references that the property
  says it requires.

- Never upload sensitive documents to this hub;
  complete applications only on a verified property
  or program website.

Your next action:
Write down the total move-in cost and required documents
before you apply.



CARD: RENTING WITH A PET

- Ask about limits on animal type, number, size, or breed.

- Ask which charges are refundable, nonrefundable, or monthly.

- Confirm vaccination, registration,
  or documentation requirements.

- Assistance animals are not pets;
  different fair-housing rules may apply.

Your next action:
Get the full pet policy and all animal-related charges
in writing before applying.



CARD: RENTERS INSURANCE

- A landlord’s policy may not cover a renter’s belongings.

- Compare personal-property, liability, temporary-living,
  and deductible terms.

- Ask what losses or hazards are excluded.

- Confirm whether the lease requires coverage
  and a minimum amount.

Your next action:
Compare coverage and exclusions,
not only the monthly price.



CARD: CHOOSING HUMAN GUIDANCE

- Housing counselors provide education
  in defined subject areas.

- Program navigators may help only within
  a particular program or referral.

- Locators, leasing professionals, and real-estate
  professionals may focus on market-rate rentals.

- Ask whether the service is free, eligibility-based,
  referral-only, or fee-based.

Your next action:
Choose the type of help that matches your task,
then review that provider’s services and terms.



CARD: STARTING WITH A HOMEOWNERSHIP CBO

- Many DC homebuyer programs direct applicants to
  participating community-based organizations.

- A CBO may provide orientation, education, counseling,
  or program application guidance.

- Participation does not guarantee program eligibility
  or mortgage approval.

- Intake steps and appointment availability
  vary by organization.

Your next action:
Open the DHCD CBO directory,
choose one participating organization,
and follow its official orientation or intake instructions.
